**Important upstream details:** v7 fixes HDMI CEC/SFR mux widths; video and DE output clocks are modeled as separately gated dividers; the `pll-ref` accepts oscillator input at 19.2, 24 or 26 MHz while producing a stable 24 MHz PLL reference. The series keeps some underdocumented bus gates at `hosc` and preserves storage clocks. These details need comparing to our tested 6.18 code, not wholesale backporting.

## Specific A733 PCK-600 comparison

The upstream A733 power-domain binding [allwinner,sun60i-a733-pck-600.h](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/master/include/dt-bindings/power/allwinner%2Csun60i-a733-pck-600.h) defines `PD_DE_SYS=1`, `PD_VO=9` and `PD_VO1=10`, matching the three domain numbers independently probed successfully on the Zero 3W with our 6.18 custom provider. **Classification:** numeric IDs verified against upstream binding; implementation and gate sequencing still NEEDS_SOURCE_DIFF. The upstream A523 PCK600 implementation is only a comparison reference, not a drop-in A733 driver. Do not modify the hardware-proven domains without reviewing code and a rollback image.

## Zero 3W HDMI clock fix cross-check

[aricshow/orangepi-zero3w-armbian-cyberdeck-mods](https://github.com/aricshow/orangepi-zero3w-armbian-cyberdeck-mods) documents a same-board DTB defect: TCON3 had only `clk_bus_tcon` and omitted its HDMI pixel-clock consumer, causing the Linux HDMI path to rely on clocks left on by vendor U-Boot. The vendor binding `CLK_HDMI_TV=252` must be translated to the **functionally equivalent** 6.18 binding (`CLK_HDMI_TV=250`, register `0x1684`) before integration. Our current RAM PCK600 DTB has **no TCON3 node**, so this is a **NEEDS_PORT requirement for the full DTB**, not an explanation proven for the existing silent-UART diagnostic boot.

Prior register matches are documented in `experiments/full-618/HDMI-CLOCK-REGISTER-MAP.md`. Differences in reset numbers, clock parents, HDMI CEC/REF handling and potential TCON pixel clock consumption must be reconciled.
